# Privacy Policy for Stacks: Budgeting & Expenses

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

Stacks does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

**Your data stays on your device.** The budgets, expenses, income and settings you enter are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. The developer has no access to them.

**No accounts, no tracking.** Stacks has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests.

**Widgets.** The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a summary of your budget from storage shared with the app on your own device. Nothing leaves your device.

**CSV export.** If you choose "Export CSV" in Settings, the file is created on your device and shared only where you send it using the system share sheet.

**Deleting your data.** Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

**Children.** Stacks does not collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.

**Contact.** Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/MPBunce/Stacks/issues
